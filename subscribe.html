How to Color-Code Your Calendar

Your calendar app colors by subscription, not by event. Subscribe to separate feeds, then assign each one a different color in your app. Here's how:

1
Tap Get My Calendar Feed above and choose your feeds.
2
Tap the 🍎 Add to Apple Calendar button — it opens Apple Calendar instantly.
3
Tap Subscribe when Apple Calendar prompts you.
4
Go to Calendars → tap ⓘ next to OtakuSync → tap Color to pick one.
5
Repeat for each platform feed. Assign each a different color — Crunchyroll orange, Netflix red, etc.
