What Is OtakuSync

Your Season Schedule,
In Your Calendar App.

OtakuSync is a live subscription calendar built for anime fans. Add it once — and every new episode, manga chapter, and season premiere automatically shows up in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or any app that supports calendar subscriptions. No app to check. No website to remember. It's just there.

📅
Live Calendar Feed

Subscribe once with a unique URL. Every update to the season schedule syncs automatically — no apps, no accounts.

🎌
Every Platform Covered

Crunchyroll, HIDIVE, Netflix, Hulu, Prime Video, Disney+ — all tracked and labeled so you know where to watch.

🔔
Episode Reminders

Your calendar app handles the notifications. Set them however you want — 30 minutes before, the morning of, whatever works.

📱
Works on Everything

iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows — if your device has a calendar, OtakuSync works on it. One tap on Apple devices.

⚡
Always Up to Date

When shows move dates, get delayed, or new titles are added mid-season, your calendar updates automatically.

📖
Manga Too

Chapter release dates included alongside episodes. Never lose track of where the anime and manga are relative to each other.

How It Looks

Your Calendar,
Upgraded.
